# Approved copy (sample brand: Acme Bio, fictional)

> Everything on this page describes a **fictional company** and exists only to show the format. Replace it
> with your own approved copy, and keep the structure.

## Which version to use

| Version | Length | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tagline | 3-6 words | Slides, social headers, signatures |
| One-liner | 1 sentence | Bios, directories, event programs |
| Short | ~50 words | Social "About", conference listings |
| Medium | ~100 words | "About" paragraph at the end of press releases |
| Long | ~200 words | Website, grants, investor and partner materials |

## Tagline

Science that holds together.

## One-liner

Acme Bio is a preclinical biotech company developing oral medicines designed to break the cross-links that
stiffen scarred tissue in fibrotic disease.

## Short (~50 words)

Acme Bio is a Boston-based biotech company developing treatments for fibrotic disease. Its lead candidate,
ACM-101, is an oral prodrug designed to break the protein cross-links that make scarred tissue stiff, with the
goal of restoring normal tissue function rather than only slowing decline.

## Medium (~100 words): press release "About"

Acme Bio is a preclinical biotech company developing small-molecule medicines for fibrotic disease, a group
of conditions in which scar tissue builds up and organs lose function. Current treatments slow progression
but do not reverse it. Acme Bio's approach targets the cross-links that hold scar tissue together. Its lead
candidate, ACM-101, is an oral prodrug that is converted to its active form in the body and is designed to
break those cross-links. Acme Bio is based in Boston, Massachusetts. Learn more at acmebio.example.

## Claims to avoid

- "Cures" or "will reverse fibrosis" → "is designed to" / "aims to" until clinical data exist.
- "First" or "only" without a source and a defined scope.
- "Safe" / "no side effects" → "well tolerated in preclinical studies".
- Any efficacy number without the model and duration.
